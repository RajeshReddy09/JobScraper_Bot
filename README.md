# n8n - Secure Workflow Automation for Job Scraping


n8n is a workflow automation platform that gives technical teams the flexibility of code with the speed of no-code. 
This workflow automates the entire process of finding relevant jobs, evaluating how well they match your résumé, and drafting tailored applications. It moves through several intelligent stages.



## ⚡ Short‑Form Workflow Descriptions


- **Trigger**: Starts the workflow when you click execute.

- **Load Candidate Profile**: Pulls your resume data from Google Sheets.

- **Fetch RemoteOK Jobs**: Retrieves the latest job listings from RemoteOK.

- **Extract Clean Jobs**: Cleans and structures the job data.

- **Filter QA SDET Jobs**: Keeps only QA/SDET‑related roles.

- **Create Candidate–Job Pairs**: Combines your profile with each job for AI scoring.

- **Limit**: Restricts how many jobs continue to the AI step.

- **Score Job Fit**: AI evaluates how well you match each job.

- **Parse AI Score**: Extracts the score and reasoning into clean fields.

- **Only Good Matches**: Filters out low‑scoring or irrelevant jobs.

- **Save Good Matches**: Saves strong matches to Google Sheets.

- **Generate Application**: AI writes a tailored application message.

- **Create Draft Email**: Creates a Gmail draft with the generated application.
